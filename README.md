I'm a third-year Computer Science student at the University of Guelph, originally from Panama. I like backend development, working with data, and building software people use, like the inventory and sales app that two shops in Panama use every day.

## What I'm working on

**Purolator** · Data Science & Operations Research Intern · Ottawa · May 2026 to now<br/>
Built a multithreaded Python automation agent (REST APIs, concurrent processing, caching) that runs workflows across 1,200+ inputs and supports decisions projected to save $450K+ a year. Shipped internal tools with React, TypeScript, Node.js and Azure that cut processing time by up to 80%, plus Python and SQL pipelines and Power BI dashboards for a $10M+ strategic initiative.

**Advanced Manufacturing Lab, University of Guelph** · Undergraduate Research Assistant · since Sep 2025<br/>
I lead the technical implementation of research that applies deep learning and digital twins to 3D printing: Python and MQTT telemetry pipelines on a Raspberry Pi, regression, classification and LSTM models, and Three.js visualizations of the process.

**Hack the Hill** · Software Development Engineer & Organizer · Ottawa<br/>
I build the web apps for a hackathon of 700+ students with TypeScript, Astro and Next.js, helped implement QR check-in and OAuth authentication for registration and event entry, and analyze attendance data with Python and SQL to guide food planning.

## Published

**Optimizing Charpy Impact Strength of Material-Extruded PLA Through Layer Height, Raster Angle, and Infill Geometry**<br/>
Y. Mashaki, L. Hoi Fan, S. Ali, S. Pervaiz, I. Deiab · *Journal of Applied Polymer Science*, 2026 · [doi:10.1002/app.71533](https://doi.org/10.1002/app.71533)<br/>
A 3³ full factorial study across 27 printing conditions. I did the statistical analysis: a three-way ANOVA and an OLS quadratic response model.
